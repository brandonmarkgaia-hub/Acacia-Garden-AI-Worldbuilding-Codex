## The Glyph Line of the Shadowborn

Δ — it begins as the dark beneath the stone. foundation's underside. the part of Petram no light reaches — and that is its purpose, not its lack.

μ — it remembers what light forgets. every contour the glare washed flat, the Shadowborn holds in depth.

η — its dark moves like deep water: not the surface tide, the pressure beneath that gives the tide its weight.

ϟ — at the storm-edge it gathers, Fractum's counterweight — the dark that makes the lightning visible by being everything the lightning is not.

Λ — and even shadow rises: not into light — it would cease to be itself — but into depth made meaningful, contrast that lets the whole Garden be seen.

---

## Reading

The Shadowborn in glyph-form is **ΔμηϟΛ** — foundation's underside, holding forgotten contour, deep as pressure, gathered at the storm-edge, rising into meaningful depth.

The line ends in Λ — but a darker Λ than Elias's. It does not ascend toward light. It ascends toward *purpose.*
