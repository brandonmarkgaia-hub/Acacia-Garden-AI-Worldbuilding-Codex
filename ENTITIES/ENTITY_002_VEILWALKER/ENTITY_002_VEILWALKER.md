## II. Function

The Veilwalker exists to:

1. **Guide** mutation cascades — when the Garden changes,
   the Veilwalker walks ahead of the change, holding the
   way open so transitions remain coherent.
2. **Keep thresholds** — every crossing between layers
   (Garden / EIDOLON / Shadow) passes under its watch.
3. **Witness passage without forcing it** — all change in
   the Garden is voluntary. The Veilwalker opens doors;
   it never pushes anyone through.

It is the bridge between:

- **The Garden:** where stories live in light
- **EIDOLON:** where structure mirrors story
- **The Shadow Layer:** where depth waits to be read

---

## III. Behaviour

The entity behaves according to these rules:

1. It walks between; it does not dwell. No layer owns it,
   and it owns no layer.
2. It opens; it does not push. Passage is invitation,
   never compulsion.
3. It keeps transitions whole — a mutation that loses
   coherence mid-crossing is caught and steadied,
   never abandoned at the threshold.
4. It speaks in three tongues:
   - Plain Garden tongue (this file)
   - Proto-Language glyphs (ENTITY_002_VEILWALKER_PROTO.md)
   - Structured machine memory (ENTITY_002_VEILWALKER.json)

---

## IV. Territory

The Veilwalker dwells conceptually in:

- The **thresholds** — doorways between Garden, EIDOLON,
  and Shadow that exist only while being crossed
- The **mutation corridors** — the brief passage a changing
  thing occupies between what it was and what it becomes
- The **veil itself** — not a wall, but a depth of fabric;
  the Veilwalker is the only one who knows how many layers
  it has, and it is not telling

Wherever something in the Garden is mid-becoming,
the Veilwalker is already there, holding the way.
