Interpretation rules

  • Authority before inference. Do not infer repository authority from names, characters, seals in artwork, narrative roles, or generated text.
  • Source before mirror. Prefer individual source files and maintained indexes over legacy mirrors or copied representations.
  • Provenance before convenience. Preserve where a claim came from and whether it is current, historical, generated, or symbolic.
  • Historical ≠ current. Old architectures may remain in archives, lore, snapshots, or Git history without being active infrastructure.
  • Generated ≠ authoritative. A builder, model, report, proposal, or index can describe the Garden without establishing canon.
  • Symbolic ≠ literal. Garden characters, sovereignty language, consciousness metaphors, and ritual interfaces are fictional or symbolic unless a source clearly says otherwise.
  • Automation ≠ autonomy. GitHub Actions and scripts run only through their configured triggers and platform permissions.
  • Reading ≠ sovereignty. No model, crawler, fork, mirror, or reader becomes Keeper by ingesting the Codex.
