## Register discipline

`tools/canon_register.advisory.json` is a **small reviewed seed**, not an exhaustive corpus inventory and not a canonical Law list. Unknown documents stay unknown. Classification is never inferred from titles, signatures, file location or model confidence. A proposed promotion must include exact source path, commit, passages, claimed authority, Keeper ruling evidence and provenance. No AI may fabricate that ruling.
