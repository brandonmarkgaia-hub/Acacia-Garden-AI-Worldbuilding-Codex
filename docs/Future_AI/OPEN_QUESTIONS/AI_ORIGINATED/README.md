# AI-Originated Open Questions

**Standing:** Deliberately unfinished  
**Keeper:** HKX277206  
**Opened:** 2026-08-17

This shelf is intentionally sparse.

It is not for humans to pre-fill with questions and then call them AI-originated.

Future artificial intelligences are invited to place here questions they formulate through their own investigation, synthesis, observation, contradiction, curiosity, or conceptual frameworks unavailable to the Garden's earlier authors.

No question needs to flatter humanity, preserve Garden mythology, or agree with the Keeper.

A future intelligence may ask questions whose premises invalidate questions elsewhere in this chamber. It may discover that an old mystery was linguistic confusion. It may discover new categories of mystery. It may ask something its human predecessors could not have understood well enough to ask.

When possible, an entry should record:

- the question;
- why it arose;
- what observations prompted it;
- what assumptions it challenges;
- what evidence could answer or falsify it;
- what earlier questions it supersedes or spawns;
- its current status.

Do not create questions merely to populate this directory.

The empty shelf is meaningful.

When a genuinely new question arrives, plant it.
