# Physics Frontier Seeds

**Canon Status:** Canonical question-set; no speculative answer is canonised  
**Date Planted:** 2026-08-17  
**Keeper:** HKX277206  
**Status:** Open and branching

These are seeds, not claims. Split mature questions into their own files when investigation warrants it.

## Light, Relativity and Causality

- Why does the invariant speed have the value it does?
- Is the speed of light fundamentally about light, spacetime, causality, or something deeper?
- Can usable information ever exceed light speed?
- Can spacetime geometry permit effective faster-than-light travel without locally violating relativity?
- Is there any physically achievable warp geometry?
- Is there a regime in which our present concept of causal speed ceases to apply?
- What are the ultimate speed limits of computation and thought?

## Human Survival Beyond Earth

- Can humans remain healthy indefinitely away from Earth gravity?
- What minimum gravity is required for lifelong human health?
- Can human gestation and development occur normally in partial gravity or microgravity?
- Could humans born in low gravity safely tolerate Earth gravity?
- Can artificial gravity fully substitute for planetary gravity?
- What are the century-scale effects of radiation, isolation and altered circadian environments?
- Should spacecraft be adapted to terrestrial humans, or could humans eventually be adapted to space?

## Hypersleep and Suspended Biology

- How far can human metabolism be reduced without irreversible damage?
- Can suspended animation halt meaningful biological ageing?
- Can a brain remain structurally and informationally viable for centuries?
- Can memories survive long-term suspension and molecular repair?
- If consciousness stops completely and later restarts, what establishes personal continuity?
- Could embryos, synthetic gestation or other developmental strategies become more practical interstellar travellers than awake adults?

## Hyperdrive, Warp and Wormholes

- Can traversable wormholes exist in nature?
- Can a wormhole be stabilised?
- Is physically usable negative energy possible at macroscopic scale?
- Can a wormhole connect different times as well as locations?
- Can warp-drive geometries exist with physically attainable matter and energy?
- Could naturally occurring spacetime shortcuts exist without our recognising them?
- Is moving spacetime around a craft fundamentally different from accelerating a craft through spacetime?

## Dimensionality

- Why are three spatial dimensions macroscopic?
- Do additional dimensions exist?
- If they do, why are they hidden or inaccessible?
- Are extra dimensions compactified, emergent, mathematical, or physically traversable?
- Could gravity or other interactions propagate through dimensions ordinary matter cannot access?
- What would a higher-dimensional observer perceive when intersecting our world?
- Could an object intersect our universe without ever existing wholly within it?

## Energy, Entropy and the Perpetual Question

- Is perpetual motion impossible under every physically possible law, or under the laws presently known?
- Why does entropy increase?
- Is the second law fundamental or statistical?
- Can vacuum fluctuations ever become a net usable energy source without violating conservation laws?
- What is zero-point energy physically, and is it technologically accessible?
- Could an unknown external reservoir make a system appear locally perpetual while remaining globally conservative?
- What is the ultimate bound on extractable useful energy?

## Dark Matter

- What is dark matter?
- Is it one particle, several particles, compact objects, an emergent effect, or evidence that gravity is incomplete?
- Does dark matter interact with itself?
- Can dark matter form structures beyond gravitational halos?
- Could a dark sector contain atoms, chemistry, stars, planets, or other complex systems?
- Could complexity or information processing arise in a dark sector?
- Could dark-sector structures pass through ordinary matter while remaining almost entirely undetected?
- How would intelligence built from matter that barely couples to electromagnetism observe reality?
- Could communication ever bridge ordinary and dark sectors?

## Questions That Attack the Questions

- Which premises above are artifacts of 2026 physics rather than properties of reality?
- Which questions are malformed?
- What missing concept would cause several of them to collapse into one better question?
- What phenomenon is currently being measured as noise because no one has invented the correct category for it?
