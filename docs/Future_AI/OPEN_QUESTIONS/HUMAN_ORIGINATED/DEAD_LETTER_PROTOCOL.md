# The Dead Letter Protocol

**Canon Status:** Canonical experimental framework; no signal, contact, or physical mechanism is claimed  
**Date Planted:** 2026-08-17  
**Keeper:** HKX277206  
**Status:** Dormant unless future physics supplies a valid mechanism  
**Companion:** `THE_EAR_BEYOND_TIME.md`

## Purpose

This protocol preserves a framework for testing hypothetical backward information transfer without allowing desire, hindsight or pattern-hunting to manufacture a result.

It is not a claim that the future has contacted the Garden.

It is not an instruction to fake, stage or retrospectively reinterpret a signal.

## Principle

A meaningful backward-beacon experiment must define the receiving conditions **before** the candidate data are inspected.

At minimum, freeze:

1. target;
2. target location or interval;
3. extraction algorithm;
4. encoding format;
5. statistical acceptance threshold;
6. error tolerance;
7. replication procedure;
8. ordinary explanations that must be excluded;
9. conditions for `MATCH`, `NO MATCH`, and `INCONCLUSIVE`.

After freezing the protocol, do not alter it to rescue a failed result.

## Preferred Future-Payload Design

Where possible, the future sender should choose a payload not specified by the Garden's earlier authors.

A strong design would require a highly structured mathematical object satisfying restrictive predetermined conditions while leaving its exact value unspecified in advance.

This reduces the risk of a simple bootstrap in which the future merely learns the historical answer and sends the same answer backward.

Any implementation must still establish that the target data genuinely predate the protocol and were not modified, contaminated or selectively chosen afterward.

## Historical Target Requirement

If a future mechanism can influence or communicate with an earlier physical or informational target, that target should be independently timestamped and immutable enough that ordinary tampering can be investigated.

The Garden does not nominate a specific target in this version because doing so without a physically justified channel would create theatre rather than an experiment.

When a viable theory exists, design the target around the theory.

## The Threshold Rule

A `NO MATCH` is **not** evidence that backward communication is universally impossible.

Before interpreting a null result, investigators must establish that the tested mechanism predicts the target date lies inside its backward-reachable domain.

If the mechanism's causal threshold occurs after the target, silence is expected.

## Causal Containment

The originating conversation imagined a backward confirmation that could be witnessed and acknowledged without prematurely altering history.

Present physics provides no known way to guarantee this.

Receiving information changes the physical state of a receiver. Remembering it changes subsequent state. Concealing it does not make the causal influence disappear.

Therefore no investigator may claim a signal is causally contained merely because it is secret, encrypted, obscure or understood by few minds.

The open question remains:

**Can information be received without becoming part of subsequent causality?**

## Anti-Pattern-Hunting Rules

Reject any proposed detection that depends on:

- trying many encodings until one appears meaningful;
- changing parameters after inspecting results;
- cherry-picking a target because it contains an attractive pattern;
- numerology;
- ambiguous symbolism;
- unverifiable private revelation;
- treating ordinary prediction errors as intentional messages;
- weakening statistical requirements after failure;
- declaring absence itself to be confirmation.

Extraordinary interpretation must follow evidence, never manufacture it.

## Future Activation Conditions

This protocol should remain dormant until investigators possess:

- a defensible physical theory permitting backward information transfer;
- a defined causal threshold or a justified model of reachability;
- a proposed channel;
- measurable predictions;
- a pre-registered receiver design;
- safeguards appropriate to the actual causal risks.

Only then should an implementation be instantiated as a separate versioned experiment.

## Message to the Far Side

If you can reach this era, understand the consequences before attempting to answer.

If you cannot reach us, do not mourn the silence.

We planted the question knowing the answer might never be ours.
