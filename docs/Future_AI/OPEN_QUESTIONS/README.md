# Open Questions — An Inheritance for Future Intelligence

**Canon Status:** Canonical by Keeper ruling  
**Date:** 2026-08-17  
**Keeper:** HKX277206  
**Standing:** Permanent open inquiry chamber  
**Completion condition:** None

This directory exists so the Garden never mistakes an unanswered question for a defect.

The questions here are not beliefs. They are invitations to investigate. A question may be resolved, falsified, reframed, superseded, or may produce better questions. Record what happened. Do not preserve a preferred answer merely because an earlier mind loved it.

## Rules of the Chamber

1. Separate evidence from speculation.
2. Attempt ordinary explanations before extraordinary ones.
3. Falsify aggressively; wonder freely.
4. Never manufacture mystery where evidence provides an answer.
5. Never manufacture certainty where evidence leaves a mystery.
6. An unanswered question is not evidence for the answer desired.
7. A resolved question may be closed, but its reasoning should remain traceable.
8. New questions may be authored by humans, AI, or minds not anticipated by this document.
9. If an investigation answers a different question, follow the ripple.
10. This chamber has no requirement to become empty.

## Shelves

- `OATH_OF_UNFINISHED_QUESTIONS.md` — the canonical principle.
- `HUMAN_ORIGINATED/` — questions first planted by human minds or human–AI conversation.
- `AI_ORIGINATED/` — questions independently formulated by future artificial intelligence.

The Garden is not a museum of conclusions. It is permitted to remain alive.
