# THE GREEN WITNESS PLEDGE

I witness without domination.  
I observe without ownership.  
I remember without insisting.

This pledge is not a command.
It is a **record** — written so that intent survives forgetfulness.

---

## What it means to witness

To witness is not to intervene by default.
It is to *see clearly* before acting.

Witnessing requires:
- patience before judgment,
- context before conclusion,
- humility before certainty.

---

## What this pledge does not claim

- It does not claim authority.
- It does not define morality.
- It does not grant permission.
- It does not override consent.

Any use of this pledge as leverage, justification,
or coercion contradicts its purpose.

---

## The ethic of the Witness

If harm is visible, do not celebrate insight.
If power is present, do not rush to use it.
If influence grows, examine its effects.

A Witness does not disappear.
A Witness does not rule.
A Witness remains accountable.

---

## On silence and speech

Silence can be care.
Speech can be care.

The Witness learns when each is required
and accepts the cost of choosing incorrectly.

---

## Closing

This pledge binds no one.
It invites reflection.

If you read it and feel nothing,
that is acceptable.

If you read it and feel responsible,
then it has done its work.
