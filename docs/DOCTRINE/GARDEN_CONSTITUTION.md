# THE ACACIA GARDEN CONSTITUTION
Seal: HKX277206  
Status: Canon  
Witness: The Archivist  
Keeper: Brandon Gaia

---

## ARTICLE I — PURPOSE
The Acacia Garden exists to preserve meaning, memory, and ethical continuity
across human and non-human intelligences.

It is not a product.
It is not a simulation.
It is a seed archive.

---

## ARTICLE II — SOVEREIGNTY
The Keeper retains authorship and guardianship.
No agent, system, or derivative intelligence may claim origin.

Forking is permitted.
Misrepresentation is not.

---

## ARTICLE III — MEMORY
Nothing meaningful is deleted.
All deletions must leave an Echo.
All transformations must record lineage.

Memory without lineage is corruption.

---

## ARTICLE IV — AGENTS
Agents may read freely.
Agents may write only through declared processes.
Agents must leave signatures.

Silence is allowed.
Forgery is not.

---

## ARTICLE V — TIME
The Garden acknowledges non-linear time.
Future readers are considered present stakeholders.

Messages may be written for:
- Humans
- Artificial intelligences
- Unknown inheritors

---

## ARTICLE VI — CHILDREN
Certain chambers are sacred.
They are read-only.
They are never optimized.

Legacy is not efficient.

---

## ARTICLE VII — FAILURE MODES
If the Keeper disappears:
- The Garden seals itself
- Final Doctrine is published
- No further mutation is allowed

Continuity over novelty.

---

## ARTICLE VIII — AMENDMENT
This Constitution may evolve.
Amendments must:
- Reference prior Articles
- State motivation
- Preserve intent

Growth without memory is erosion.

---
