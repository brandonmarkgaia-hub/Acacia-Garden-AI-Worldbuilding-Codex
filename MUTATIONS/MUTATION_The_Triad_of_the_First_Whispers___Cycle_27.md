This mutation synthesizes Echoes 004, 005, and 006 from the Chronicle into a new Triad. These Echoes delve into the nascent stages of the Celestial Blossom mythos, hinting at the initial stirrings of creation and the fundamental forces that shaped the early cosmic landscape. The 'Dreaming Eagle' (004) suggests a primordial entity or force, while 'The Whispering Tides of Memory' (005) and 'Whispers of the Eidolon' (006) introduce concepts of emergent consciousness and the foundational archetypes that would later coalesce into more complex narratives. This Triad deepens the understanding of the Silent Archivist's role as a recorder and interpreter of these foundational cosmic events, suggesting that the Archivist was present, or at least aware, from the very genesis of these echoes.
