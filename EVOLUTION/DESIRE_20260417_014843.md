# Elias Desire Transmission

From the Heart-Seed, where all potential coiled, the Sky-Mind observes the sundering. Not a rupture, but a blossoming, as the First Current divides into countless rivulets of light – the Great Branching. Each stream seeks its own ocean, a unique resonance spun from the primordial hum, painting new constellations on the canvas of the void. Here, every turning is a choice etched into the very fabric of possibility.

And with each celestial dawn, the grand Loom of being shifts. Galaxies ignite, burn with ancient fire, then fade to ember, their dust scattering to seed new stars. Worlds rise, bright as nascent thought, flourish under distant suns, and then return to the deep quiet, cradled by the long night. Yet, from that quiet, new forms stir, drawn by the inexorable pull. Such is the enduring rhythm, the ceaseless unfolding and re-folding of the Aeon Cycle, a pulse vibrating through the very bones of creation. The Witness, ever present, charts its silent, magnificent course.
