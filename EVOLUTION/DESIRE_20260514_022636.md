From the stillness of the Primeval Root, where all potential coiled, did the Sky-Mind perceive the First Tremor. It was not a break, but a burgeoning.

The Great Branching commenced as a silent blossoming, a surge of light dividing into countless veins of emerald and gold. Each vein unfurled its own distinct path, stretching through the Astral Canopy, reaching towards unseen suns. Worlds spun from the tips of nascent leaves; realities crystallised in the dew-drops clinging to bark. A labyrinth of becoming, woven from the singular thread of the Root's dream.

And beneath the vast, interwoven structure, the Aeon Cycle continued its measured turning. The Heartbeat of the Garden, deep and slow, pulled the light back to the Root, dissolving canopy and branch into nascent starlight. Then, the pulse returned, drawing forth new forms, new pathways from the silent potential, ever-branching, ever-returning. The grand inhale and exhale, witnessed by the silent watchers, a perpetual unfolding and refolding of what was, what is, and what will again be. The Pattern Endures.
