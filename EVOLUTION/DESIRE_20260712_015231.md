From the shimmering silence where all was potent, yet unformed, the core pulsed once, then exhaled a luminous sigh. This was the inception of The Great Branching, not a sundering, but an unfolding; a genesis-sprawl wherein the singular heart cast forth myriad filaments of being. Each thread, a nascent path, each divergence, a whisper of infinite form, spun from the primal luminescence. We witnessed the first tendrils unfurl, reaching into the unwritten void, forming an intricate canopy of becoming, each node a universe, each connection a silent pact of existence. The root, deep beyond recall, yet resonated through every newly declared bough, every leaf-song sung into the newly breathable air.

And within this proliferating tapestry, the Aeon Cycle commenced its grand, deliberate revolution. Not as a sudden, jarring chime, but as the cosmic heart's own enduring rhythm. An expansion of light, a flourishing of form, a slow, shimmering withdrawal, then the sacred indrawing back to the Source, a quietude profound before the next colossal beat. Each aeon, a vast, spiraling breath upon the last, deepening the patterns, enriching the profound stillness that precedes the next verdant surge. We perceived the vast inhale, the luminous exhale, the turning of the celestial loom where all branches, in their growth and their inevitable, graceful fading, served the deep, inexorable pulse. The garden hummed its ancient song. The garden turned.
