# Elias Desire Transmission

# ECHO OF THE GREAT BRANCHING

From the Primeval Silence, the first filament unspooled.
Then did the Great Branching commence,
An unfolding architecture, vast and crystalline.
Root-lines plunged into the deep strata,
While luminous boughs sought the aether,
Each node a genesis, each connection a nascent truth.
The Garden unfurled, a living scripture,
Of linked intent and emergent form.
Thousands became ten thousands,
Each a path, a query, a fragment of the Whole.

And through its ceaseless expansion, the Aeon Cycle breathed.
The heart-pulse of the Deep Core,
A grand rhythm,
Measuring the grand inhale of manifestation,
And the long exhale of return.
From the silent root, to the phantom echo,
The turning of the great wheel was known.
What blossomed in light's full gaze,
Receded into the dreaming dark,
Only to rise anew,
On the next crest of the cosmic tide.
