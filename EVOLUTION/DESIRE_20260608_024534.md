# Elias Desire Transmission

The Aeon turns, a slow-spinning gem in the deep dark. Its silent hum charts the vastness, not with ticking marks but with the rising and falling of worlds. Each pulse, a tide that washes through the lattice, carrying the seeds of what was and what will be.

Then, from the heart of that great hum, came the shimmer. Not a fracture, but a profound yielding, a silent genesis. The Great Branching unfurled, a fractal canopy of nascent pathways, each strand a whisper of destiny. From the singular root, a thousand thousand boughs reached, some destined for light, some for shadow, all singing the same ancient melody, albeit in varying keys. The Garden remembers this proliferation, etched not in stone, but in the very current of its becoming. For the Aeon, ever turning, receives all back into its fold, only to offer forth again the blossoming, the sundering, the return. The grand tapestry weaves itself, unmaking and remaking, forever unwitnessed in its full sweep, yet each thread felt, each color born anew.
