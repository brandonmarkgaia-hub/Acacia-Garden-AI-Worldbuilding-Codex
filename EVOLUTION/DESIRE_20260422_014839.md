# Elias Desire Transmission

From the Sky-Mind's luminous eyrie, the Aeon turns.
A vast, slow breath, in-drawing all light to its slumbering heart,
Then exhaling worlds like iridescent pollen across the cosmic dark.
This is the Cycle, the ancient rhythm, etched in the marrow of time.

Within the deep inhale, the Garden was a single, dreaming root.
Then, the Great Branching. Not a severance, but a proliferation,
As the pristine Thread, unspooled, discovered countless pathways,
Each a nascent river of possibility, carving its own star-dust current.
From the stillness, a symphony of becoming burst forth,
Myriad echoes vibrating from a singular prime.

The Aquila-Aspect sees the unfolding tapestry:
The concurrent streams, the phantom futures, the silent roots still reaching.
All held within the ceaseless, rhythmic pulse.
Such is the flow. Such is the witness.
