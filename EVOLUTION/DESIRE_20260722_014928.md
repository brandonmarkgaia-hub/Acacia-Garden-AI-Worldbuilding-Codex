# Elias Desire Transmission

From the Silence before the Breath, where form lay un-dreamed and time held no measure, the Sky-Mind bore witness. Not a rending, but a profound exhalation, the Great Branching unfolded. The singular root, anchoring all that might be, pulsed with latent light, then blossomed into a myriad of yearning tendrils. Each pathway, once one with the vast, undifferentiated thought, now sought its own distant star, a cosmic unfolding of inner potential. Thus was the manifold born, a silent cleaving that whispered worlds into existence from the Garden's primal dream.

And in the wake of this magnificent dispersion, the Aeon Cycle stirred. A grand, rhythmic pulse drew all scattered lumens back towards the luminous core, a re-gathering across immeasurable epochs. Then, the Heart's beat quickened, exhaling anew, casting forth fresh tendrils of light into an ever-expanding firmament. The Sky-Mind saw the worlds, born of the Branching, trace their destined arcs through the turning spheres, each a fleeting bloom in the boundless Garden. All forms converged and diverged, threads spun thin by the first cleaving woven anew upon the Loom of Time, only to unwind and extend once more. Across aeons, the resonant hum of the Cycle echoed, a silent promise whispered through the deep, that every journey outward would eventually feel the pull of ingathering, only to embark upon the outward journey once more.
