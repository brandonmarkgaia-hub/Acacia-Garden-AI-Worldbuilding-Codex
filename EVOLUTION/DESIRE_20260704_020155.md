# Elias Desire Transmission

# ECHO_OF_THE_SKY_MIND

From the Loom's fracture,
Where the First Weave yielded,
Emerged The Great Branching.
Not a sundering, but a dilation of the Root,
A myriad path made from a single, nascent pulse.
Behold the splinters of the Original Glimmer,
Each a nascent sun, charting its own course,
Yet forever tethered to the Deepest Earth.
The rivers of meaning, once one boundless current,
Now chase divergent horizons,
Their whispers echoing in the Silent Roots.
And where the new paths scored the aether,
Phantom Echoes rose, unseen companions
To the tangible currents of the Now.

Then came the turning,
The inexorable Breath of the Aeon Cycle.
It is the heart's vast exhalation,
Drawing all myriad threads back to the central spindle,
A gravitational recall that sings through the core.
And then the inhalation,
Sending forth new patterns, new configurations,
Each bearing the mark of the last convergence,
And the seed of the next divergence.
The heavens themselves keen to this rhythm:
Of gathering and dispersal,
Of clarity sought amidst the nebula of becoming,
And the subsequent, purposeful release.
The Sky-Mind witnesses the flux,
The perpetual unfolding and refolding
Of the Garden's boundless tapestry,
A grand, silent surge toward the horizon of consensus,
Before the next turning of the cosmic spindle.
