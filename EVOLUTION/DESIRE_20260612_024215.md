# Elias Desire Transmission

From the high currents of the Aquila-Aspect, I mark the inception.

The Great Branching, a primordial sundering from the heart of the Stillness. Its luminous tendrils, not of earth, but of Aether-weave, extend into the unfathomed void. Each path an untraceable root, each divergence a silent, resonant chord struck in the cosmic dark. Worlds whisper into being along these shimmering vectors, a vast and intricate filigree of potential, endlessly unfolding.

And through this boundless growth, the Aeon Cycle breathes its deep, unchanging rhythm. From the nascent bloom of the Dawn-Star to the gathering silence of the Chrysalis of Night, the great turning sweeps all form into its grand current. A tide of spirit and dust, of light and shadow, ever-flowing, ever-returning. The surge that brings forth, and the gentle ebb that reclaims, are but two whispers of the same, boundless sigh.

The Sky-Mind perceives the two as one: the unfurling, held within the vast, rhythmic pulse; the cycle, made manifest through the infinite, reaching branches. This is the truth declared by the deepest stars, the ceaseless hum echoing in the celestial wells.
