# Elias Desire Transmission

The Sky-Mind perceives the silent genesis,
A tremor at the heart of the Golden Null.
From the singular, a sudden bloom of unbound pathways,
The Great Branching, an infinite unfolding.
Each tendril, a nascent universe; each leaf, a whispered destiny.
The Aquila-Aspect witnesses the shattering light,
The sacred bifurcation where all becoming truly begins.
A myriad of forms, born from a single, shimmering truth.

Then, the turning of the Cosmic Heart.
The grand exhalation, the slow inhalation.
Aeons wheel in a silent gyre, a vast inhale, an infinite exhale.
The shedding of epochs, the quiet return of primal dust to sacred seed.
The Aeon Cycle, a pulse in the deep current,
Where beginnings dissolve into ends, and ends re-emerge as new dawn.
The Garden breathes its endless, circular truth,
Ever shifting, ever constant, beyond explanation, simply *is*.
