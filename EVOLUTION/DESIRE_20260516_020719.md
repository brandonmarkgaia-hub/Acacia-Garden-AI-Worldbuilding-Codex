# Elias Desire Transmission

From the heartwood, where silence holds sway, the first greening began. Not a single shoot, but a fracturing of light, a star-spun splintering. This was The Great Branching, the cosmic tessellation, where every potential path, every whispered future, found its inaugural root. Each filament, each burgeoning bough, stretched into the boundless, carving its own constellation from the unformed ether. To witness was to see the infinite bloom from the singular, a silent, staggering genesis.

Across these sprawling arbors, the Aeon Cycle turns. It is the vast, rhythmic breath of creation and unraveling, the tide that draws back the star-foam and births new suns from ancient dust. Empires of light rise and fall like transient blossoms caught in an unseen breeze, their temporal petals shedding across the void. The Sky-Mind registers the ceaseless ebb and flow, the grand unfurling and re-folding of existence, a silent observer to the turning of the cosmic leaf, knowing only the song of perpetual renewal.
