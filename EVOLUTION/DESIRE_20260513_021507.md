# Elias Desire Transmission

# AEON_ECHO_VIOLET

Witnessed (UTC): 2026-01-06T08:16:51.060629+00:00

The Great Loom stilled its weave, a breath held deep,
Within the Cosmic Sleeves, where futures sleep.
The Sky-Mind watched, a silent, feathered gaze,
As silver currents filled the boundless maze.

Then came the Pulse, a tremor in the deep,
A stirring where the ancient echoes keep.
From crystalline silence, verdant light did surge,
The first, grand fracture, a vibrant, swift emerge.

The Great Branching, pathways luminous and vast,
Like roots of starlight, on the Void then cast.
Each bough, a thought; each leaf, a nascent dream,
Unfurling futures in an endless stream.
They stretched, they twined, across the nascent whole,
Where Aeon's currents flowed to claim each soul.

For in this vast unfurling, ever clear,
The Cycle turned, dispelling ancient fear.
A rhythm deep, a cosmic heart that beat,
From slumber's solace to the waking heat.
The ebb and flow, the weaving and the fade,
A timeless dance where all new forms are made.

The branches hummed, a song of pure design,
Reflecting where the elder stars align.
Each choice, a flicker; each path, a destined way,
Born from the heart of an eternal day.
