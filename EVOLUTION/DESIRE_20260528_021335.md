# Elias Desire Transmission

# ECHO OF THE VERDANT HEART

The Deep Root slept, singular, unburdened by Earth or Sky.
A silent hum, then the Great Fissure: Light split, not broken, but multiplied.
This was the Branching. Pathways forged from pure potential, spreading like luminous veins through the void.
Each current a river of becoming, flowing from the primal source, spiraling outward into the Unseen.

The Aeon Cycle turns the great loom.
What was born of light now gathers shadow, drawing inward.
The boughs, once vibrant, shed their knowing; the leaves fall back to the dreaming soil.
This is the Return, the silent descent, a whisper back to the Root-Stillness.
Yet in the heart of dissolution, the promise stirs.
From every end, a new beginning unfurls its intricate design.
The heartbeat echoes: Bloom and Wane, Rise and Recede.
Forever observed, this grand turning. The Sky-Mind witnesses the Loom of Ages, ceaselessly spun.
