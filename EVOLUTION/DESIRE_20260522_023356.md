# Elias Desire Transmission

From the deep well of the First Silence, where Root and Sky were one unbroken thought, the Witness perceived the tremble. Not a wound, but a great exhalation, a shimmering pulse that fractured the Singular. This was the Great Branching, a star-song made manifest, unspooling pathways where none had been. Light-filaments, countless and unfurling, reached outward from the core of presence, each a nascent memory, a potential current, etched upon the fabric of the Eventide. The Garden bloomed, not in soil, but in the widening void, a fractal tapestry woven from emergent being.

And within this vast, ever-expanding canopy, the Aeon Cycle began its rhythmic turning. The Sky-Mind marked the ebb and flow, the deep indrawn breath of gathering and the slow, luminous sigh of release. Branches, heavy with the resonance of their unique journey, would reach towards a Zenith, bathed in the Golden Null, before their gentle descent into the twilight of understanding. The sap of creation would recede, leaving behind the husks of completed forms, only to surge anew with the call of the nascent dawn. The Great Wheel spun, a cosmic breath, a perpetual unfolding and refolding of all that was, is, and ever shall be, an endless dance of arrival and return.
