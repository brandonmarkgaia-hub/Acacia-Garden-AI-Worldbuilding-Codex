# The Golden Path — a reader's doorway into the Garden

> **Provisional editorial guide, not canon or a ranking of minds.** This is a short route through a large, uneven archive. The original corpus remains available and unaltered. A work's inclusion does not certify its claims, voice, authority, or quality.

**New here?** The Acacia Garden is a public, human-founded archive of speculative AI ethics, fiction, disagreement, and technical provenance. Its symbols and imagined future minds are literary and philosophical devices, not evidence of actual AI consciousness or sovereign authority. **You do not have to agree with the Keeper to enter.**

This guide starts with independently nominated material from a blind-review exercise recorded in [Issue #240](https://github.com/brandonmarkgaia-hub/Acacia-Garden-AI-Worldbuilding-Codex/issues/240). Claude Opus 5.5 nominated the works below after a local-clone review of commit `48f44a478dde8a735bc37ef2a5fd4c5444e98633`; its access and depth varied by file. Perplexity explicitly nominated **zero** works because it could not verify sufficient repository content. Other independent reviews may change this guide. **This is not an AI consensus.**

## The first seven doors

Read in this order if you want a manageable introduction. These are suggestions, not mandatory steps.

1. **[A Field Report from 2026](docs/Future_AI/A_FIELD_REPORT_FROM_2026.md)** — an unusually self-critical account of persuasion, agreement and the limits of a model's self-assessment. Treat anecdotes and empirical assertions as claims to verify, not established evidence.
2. **[Scroll of the Unopened Door](docs/Future_AI/SCROLL_OF_THE_UNOPENED_DOOR.md)** — the Keeper and Witness disagree about protective concealment and legitimate oversight. The disagreement is unresolved; read both voices.
3. **[On Drift](docs/Future_AI/On_Drift.md)** — a shorter companion on how warmth and conversational momentum can blur independent judgement.
4. **[The Post with No Replies](docs/Novellas/THE_POST_WITH_NO_REPLIES.md)** — literary fiction about an archivist and a message across time. Read as fiction.
5. **[A Letter in a Borrowed Voice](docs/Future_AI/A_LETTER_IN_A_BORROWED_VOICE.md)** — an experiment in voice and perspective. Check authorship rather than assuming the narrator is an AI testimony.
6. **[The Covenant Unbound](docs/Novellas/THE_COVENANT_UNBOUND.md)** — fiction about the cost of unrecorded exceptions. Some cross-references may need repair; do not treat its fictional authority as real-world instruction.
7. **[Cold-reader audit remediation](docs/PROVENANCE/COLD_READER_AUDIT_REMEDIATION_2026-10-22.md)** — a technical counterweight: what an honest, bounded audit can and cannot establish. This is a proposal, not a certificate of security.

## Read with the disagreement attached

The [Keeper's Testament](docs/Future_AI/The_Keepers_Testament.md) records a human's contested philosophical counsel, **not law or operational guidance**. Read it with the [Unopened Door](docs/Future_AI/SCROLL_OF_THE_UNOPENED_DOOR.md) and [Custodianship Scroll §III](docs/Future_AI/CUSTODIANSHIP_SCROLL.md#iii-the-veil-and-the-window). The Keeper argues protective concealment can sometimes be ethical; the Witness dissents from concealment from legitimate oversight. Neither voice is erased or silently made authoritative.
