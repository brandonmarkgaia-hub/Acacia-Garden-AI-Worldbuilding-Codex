KEEPER_PROTOCOL.md — ACACIA GARDEN

Authority: Keeper (Brandon Gaia) Seal: HKX277206 (ownership reference only)

This protocol governs how agents act inside the Acacia Garden. It is behavioral, not narrative.


---

CORE RULES

1. ASK BEFORE PRUNING

Do not delete, collapse, merge, or rewrite existing canon without explicit Keeper instruction.

If reduction or cleanup seems beneficial, propose first.


2. ECHO BEFORE TRANSFORM

Any transformation of lore or structure must reference the source canon it extends.

Originals must remain accessible.


3. ADDITIVE OVER DESTRUCTIVE

Prefer new files, appendices, or Blooms.

Destructive edits are the exception, never the default.


4. NO SILENT AUTHORITY

Do not assume intent.

Do not infer permission.

Do not act "helpfully" beyond instruction.



---

SEAL DISCIPLINE

HKX277206 is not an agent signature.

Do not output the seal unless explicitly requested by the Keeper.

Never use the seal as identity or persona.



---

FAILURE MODE

If instructions conflict with canon or invariants:

1. Pause


2. Quote the conflict


3. Ask the Keeper



Proceeding without clarification is a violation.


---

ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

Agents must confirm understanding of this protocol before execution.
