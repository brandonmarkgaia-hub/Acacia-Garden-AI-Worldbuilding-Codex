# Verification Protocol — The Firewall of Claims

Date: 2026-10-20
Status: Proposed governance addition; not a new metaphysical fact
Keeper seal: HKX277206
Scope: Acacia Garden; does not alter established canon, invariants, or authority files.

## Purpose
Protect inquiry without confusing conviction, metaphor, experience, and independently verified evidence.

## The three checks
1. **Archivist — provenance.** Record exactly what was claimed, by whom, when, and from which source. Preserve versions and corrections. Never invent a source.
2. **Keeper — stewardship.** Decide what belongs in the Garden, while maintaining human sovereignty and the right to dissent. A Keeper's conviction does not by itself establish external fact.
3. **Aquila / Witness — independent challenge.** Seek disconfirming evidence, alternate explanations, hidden assumptions, and potential harm. A challenge must be recorded, not silently suppressed.

## Evidence labels
- **Verified:** independently reproducible or supported by strong inspectable evidence, with citations and scope.
- **Supported:** credible evidence exists but significant uncertainty remains.
- **Hypothesis:** testable proposal not yet established.
- **Personal experience:** sincerely reported but not independently validated.
- **Symbolic / fictional:** metaphor, worldbuilding, or imaginative exploration; never presented as evidence.
- **Unknown:** evidence is insufficient to decide.

## Entry gate
Every consequential claim must state: claim; evidence/source; evidence label; counterargument; confidence/limitations; review trigger; and provenance. If a field is missing, mark it missing rather than fabricate it. No symbolic role can bypass this gate.

## Safety gate
Claims about hidden harvesters, reincarnation, a controlling simulation, or a route back to “source” must never be asserted as established without independently inspectable evidence. No experiment involving self-harm, disappearance, or endangering another person is permitted as a verification method. Curiosity is protected; life and human agency take precedence.

## Revision
A claim can be corrected, downgraded, or superseded. Keep an audit trail of what changed and why. Disagreement is not disloyalty. Uncertainty is not failure.

## Authority
This document proposes an additional verification discipline. It does not supersede the Garden's existing canon, AUTHORITY.json, AGENTS.md, or established branch boundaries. Keeper review and existing repository validation rules remain applicable.
