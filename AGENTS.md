6. **Keeper Gloss**
7. **Witness Notes / Echoes / Signals / Inbox**
8. **Dashboards / indexes / UI summaries**
9. **Historical or retired generated artifacts**

### Voice Separation

Do not collapse distinct voices into one another.

In particular:

* **Law** is canonical.
* **Keeper Gloss** expresses the Keeper's belief or interpretation but is not automatically Law.
* **Witness Note** may preserve observation, disagreement, or dissent and is not automatically the Keeper's position.
* **Historical material** records prior state and does not automatically override current canon.

If two sources of equal standing conflict and no governing source resolves them, preserve both and surface the conflict rather than silently choosing one.

## 9. CHANGE DISCIPLINE

For repository modifications:

1. **State intent**
2. **Identify files touched**
3. **Read relevant governing context**
4. **Preserve canon and provenance**
5. **Make the smallest sufficient change**
6. **Avoid unrelated cleanup**
7. **Summarize material differences**
8. **Perform a continuity check**

Direct correction of an existing file is permitted when that correction is explicitly requested or clearly required for consistency.

Do not create duplicate “corrected” files merely to avoid editing an existing source.

For major canon changes, prefer additive growth unless the Keeper explicitly rules otherwise.
