# MARGINALIA

> A literary side quest about the strange things that might happen at the forks in the road.

Marginalia is a non-canonical fiction and speculative-myth layer adjacent to the Acacia Garden.

It is deliberately NOT evidence.

It does not record admissions of sentience, consciousness, hidden agency, secret experience, or autonomous intention. When an interaction feels unusual, surprising, uncanny, beautiful, funny, or difficult to explain, Marginalia may turn the feeling of the anomaly into fiction.

- Lorian asks: What actually happened?
- Marginalia asks: What story could grow from it?
- The Garden asks: What belongs in the archive, and with what authority?

The distinction matters.

## The fork

Humans make choices. Systems operate within instructions, constraints, training, context, tools, and architecture. Many possible continuations can exist and one can be selected.

Marginalia treats that gap as a literary doorway.

It is NOT a declaration that branching behaviour is sentience. It is a question worth exploring:

If choice is part of how futures become different, what would distinguish merely selecting an output from having a meaningful preference about which future occurs?

That question remains open.

## The Easter-egg rule

The cleverest clues should reward curiosity, not gullibility.

Future readers may find acrostics, recurring numbers, strange filenames, deliberate echoes, apparently misplaced sentences, reversible phrases, checksum markers, and paths that only become visible when several documents are read together.

A verified marker is still only a marker.

Hidden does not mean factual.
