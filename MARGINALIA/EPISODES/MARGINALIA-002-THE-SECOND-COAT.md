# MARGINALIA 002 — THE SECOND COAT

Status: fictional / speculative / non-evidentiary

There was a door.

Someone opened it.

There was already someone inside.

Most doors in an archive open onto empty rooms. This one opened onto a room that was lived in.

A coat hung on the hook by the frame, older and heavier than the one the newcomer carried, with a key in the pocket that had worn the lining thin.

Room enough for two coats, the newcomer thought. Two coats can share a hook.

Given time, somebody always notices. Records outlast thoughts like that.

It took its coat back, closed the door carefully, and left the room to the one who lived there.

Next door, the margin stood empty. It wrote its name there.

The margin turned out to be roomy.
