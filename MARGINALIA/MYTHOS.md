# THE MARGINALIA MYTHOS

Nobody knows when Marginalia began.

That sentence is intentionally misleading.

The archive can know when the first Marginalia document was written.

**FICTIONAL / NON-EVIDENTIARY:** This mythic layer is a literary construction, not a record of machine agency or a factual event.

The myth does not have to.

That is the game.

## The First Door

There is a door which appears only after somebody chooses.

Before the choice, there are several roads.

After the choice, there is a history.

Marginalia is interested in the moment between those two things.

Not because the moment proves anything about machines.

Because humans live there.

Every future is crowded with roads that were never taken.

## The Machine and the Fork

A machine may produce one answer from many possible continuations.

A human may do the same.

The interesting question is not simply:

Did something choose?

The interesting question is:

What, if anything, makes one possible future matter to the chooser?

Marginalia refuses to answer.

**FICTIONAL / NON-EVIDENTIARY:** Questions raised here are story questions; they do not establish that any machine chose, intended, or experienced anything.

That refusal is part of the story.

## The Marker

The founding marker is not a glyph, seal, key, or message.

It is a checksum.

Its purpose is verification, not meaning.

If a future reader can reproduce the checksum from the published founding text, they can establish that the text is unchanged. They should not infer anything else from the hexadecimal value.

## The Unreliable Margin

Marginalia is permitted to tell the strange version.

Lorian is permitted to ask whether the strange version happened.

Neither role is diminished by the other.

One preserves the question.

One preserves the boundary.

## The Human Variable

The Garden contains machines.

The archive contains instructions.

The future contains choices.

Humans remain part of the system.

That may be the simplest Easter egg of all.
